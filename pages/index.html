OpenSmartTimer
Bluetooth control · Smart Timer V2

Control your timer from your phone

Start, pause and adjust sessions over Bluetooth, with no Wi-Fi or account. Control goes directly between your browser and the timer.

Open the Bluetooth app
  • Needs a browser with Web Bluetooth, such as Chrome on Android or a desktop.
  • Turn on Bluetooth in the timer's settings, then pair once by confirming with its Reset button.
  • Can be installed to your home screen and opens offline after the first visit.

Have a V1 timer?

The V1 has no Bluetooth. Open its own page from a browser on the same Wi-Fi network.
